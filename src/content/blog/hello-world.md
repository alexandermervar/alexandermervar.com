Welcome to my blog. I'll be writing here about things that interest me — projects I'm working on, ideas I'm exploring, and whatever else seems worth sharing.

## Why a blog?

I've found that writing is one of the best ways to clarify my own thinking. If something I write ends up being useful to someone else, that's a bonus.

## What to expect

Posts will vary in length and frequency. Some will be long-form pieces; others will be short notes. The RSS feed is the best way to follow along.

Thanks for reading.
